## Definitions (fixed before the run)

- Price: power de-vig of the snapshot's own `book`. Snapshots every 2 min in play.
- Features: d2, abs_d2, d6, d10, vol10, spread, minutes. d2, d6, d10 are de-vigged probability moves over 2, 6 and 10 min; vol10 is the sd of the five 2-min moves; spread is the relative back/lay width; minutes is time since the first in-play snapshot.
- Label: p(book_10m) - p(book_60s) at the same snapshot. Both are S58 look-ahead books; neither is a feature.
- Walk-forward: matches in order of first in-play snapshot. Training rows must have their 10-min label closed before the test match starts. A fold needs at least 100 training rows from 3 matches; other folds make no prediction.
- Models: random walk (no change), momentum and reversion (sign of d6 times the mean absolute label), ridge (alpha 1.0), gradient boosting ({'n_estimators': 60, 'max_depth': 2, 'learning_rate': 0.05, 'subsample': 0.8, 'min_samples_leaf': 10}).
