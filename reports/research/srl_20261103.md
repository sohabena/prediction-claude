### Verdict rule

- `feasible` only if there are at least 300 settled SRL matches with a closing price AND the 95% confidence interval of the absolute calibration bias (observed win rate minus mean de-vigged closing probability, Wilson interval) lies entirely above the median closing margin.
- Otherwise `not feasible yet`, with the number of further settled SRL matches still needed: the larger of the shortfall to 300 and, when the point estimate of the bias exceeds the margin, the sample size at which the bias interval would clear the margin. That sample size is optimistic because it uses the point estimate.
