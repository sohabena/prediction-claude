## Story

As the data engineer, I need a robust, officially sanctioned pipeline to import official, free Basic-tier historical data archives directly downloaded from `historicdata.betfair.com` into TimescaleDB, so that our models have access to real exchange market ticks and closing prices without web scraping or terms-of-service violations.

## Background

- Synthetic odds generated by `scripts/synthetic_market.py` served as an initial bootstrap for offline RL training, but real alpha requires exposure to authentic market spreads, overrounds, and traded volume.
- Betfair provides official, free 1-minute historical data archives to registered users under its Developer Program terms.
- PHOENIX already has the core parsing logic in [`scraper/parsers/betfair_historical.py`](file:///c:/Users/sohai/Downloads/prediction-claude/scraper/parsers/betfair_historical.py).
- We need to harden [`scripts/import_betfair_data.py`](file:///c:/Users/sohai/Downloads/prediction-claude/scripts/import_betfair_data.py) to automatically unpack `.tar.bz2` / `.json.gz` archives, validate match coverage, normalize team names to PHOENIX canonical formats, and batch-insert into TimescaleDB `odds_ticks` and `match_training_status`.
